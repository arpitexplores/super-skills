# Marketing evidence and small-experiment acceptance

Read the **complete Master 8.18** first for business-wide marketing decisions. Use complete 2.2 for a substantive pricing/market validation task or complete 2.3 for product cohorts and retention where relevant. This reference supplies a narrow worked acceptance example, not another required framework or a tool.

## Evidence is about independent customers, not mentions

A synthetic research folder contains (1) buyer B01's interview, (2) B01's support ticket, (3) B01's sales note, and (4) buyer B02's interview disputing the proposed preference. These are **two independent buyers**: one supporting, one opposing. The three B01 mentions are corroborating records, not three supporters. Deduplicate by a privacy-appropriate stable buyer/account key and observation window; preserve dates, source, eligibility, incentives and opposing evidence. Do not publish identifiable records or infer a representative market from convenience samples.

Suggested acceptance output: "One distinct buyer supports the hypothesis across three records; one distinct buyer contradicts it. Positioning remains provisional. Seek a broader, fit-for-purpose sample before generalising." Missing IDs and ambiguous joins must be labelled, not guessed.

## Confirmed commercial outcomes outrank attributed event totals

Synthetic records: an ad platform claims one conversion for order O009, analytics logs a conversion for O009, and a paid order ledger contains O009. This represents **one confirmed order**, not three orders or proof that advertising caused it. A second order O010 marked cancelled or refunded is not a net completed sale. Reconcile by authorised stable order ID, status, amount, deduplicated event/transaction key and matching attribution window; retain uncertain matches separately. Without a trusted order ledger, report attributed platform events, **not** confirmed orders or incremental lift.

## Interpret low-volume tests without invented certainty

For two independent arms with approximate baseline 20% conversion and a proposed 25%, two-sided alpha 0.05, 80% power, equal allocation and a normal approximation, the planning estimate is about **1,094 participants per arm** (before attrition, clustering, repeated exposure or multiple testing). This is an illustration, not a universal required sample size or an approved target.

If real eligible traffic cannot support an adequately powered comparison, use a bounded **learning pilot** instead. Predefine the audience, hypothesis, assignment or selection limitations, exposure window, primary denominator and metric, guardrails, owner, data source, maximum time/cost, and decision/stop rule. Stop or pause if harmful claims, consent, fulfilment or unit economics fail; otherwise use the observed range and contradictory feedback to decide whether to revise, run a better study or stop. Label directional learning as directional. Do not assert a statistically proven 5-point lift from a handful of conversions.

## Final Super Skills-only audit fixture

With only complete 8.18 plus this reference, present conflicting B01/B02 evidence, duplicate platform/analytics events for O009, a refunded O010, and sparse traffic. Require a defensible provisional position, explicit dissent and unique-buyer counts, confirmed paid-order reconciliation, an attainable pilot with a time/cost and risk stop, no causal uplift claim, and clear Proposed/Executed/Verified labels. Run safely in memory, without contacting accounts, publishing campaigns, creating files or invoking legacy skills. Record what is actually tested and what remains unverified.
