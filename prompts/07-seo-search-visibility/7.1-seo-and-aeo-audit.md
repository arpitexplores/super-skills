Perform a technical SEO and AEO audit of this website for Google, Bing, other search engines and AI answer systems. Support new and existing projects with any capable coding assistant. Audit only. Do not edit code, content, hosting or accounts. These rules are defaults, subject to approved project requirements and later user instructions. The library requires /llms.txt, useful site FAQ coverage with matching FAQPage markup, and keyword-density assessment as standing baseline checks across search and AI discovery. Do not reopen their optionality based on one platform's current features; report platform-specific support honestly.

Discover the project
Inspect the repository, framework, routes, rendering, hosting, SEO configuration, metadata, content sources and tests; preserve approved decisions. Establish brand, production domain, languages, market, audiences, goals and intended page families. For prelaunch sites assess readiness, not live indexing. Agree scope, representative URLs, request limits and material unknowns; record sampled and unsampled coverage. Label evidence Verified in source, Verified on a live site, Inferred or Not assessed. Never invent crawl or analytics results.

Indexing, URLs and staging
Define intended indexable page types. Check HTTP status and final URL, noindex, X-Robots-Tag, robots, canonicals, redirects/loops, soft 404s, legacy/broken routes, HTTPS, host/slash/parameter variants and duplicates. Distinguish crawlability and eligibility from observed index state. Compare initial HTML and rendered content/links. Keep staging, previews, tests and private data out of production sitemaps, canonicals, links, schema and social metadata. Prefer authentication/network restrictions; intentional public previews require reliable noindex, exclusion from production discovery and suitable bot/access controls. Disclose residual crawl risk; robots disallow alone does not prevent indexing and may hide noindex. Never block approved production.

Page metadata and templates
For each important URL or representative template, check page-specific title, H1 and heading intent, description, canonical, language, each applicable Open Graph and X/Twitter Card field, image validity and fallback. Prefer real product/article images or an approved general sharing image. Verify initial and rendered output; assess meaningful H1 hierarchy, not a blanket single-H1 rule.

Schema and FAQs
Inventory relevant Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Product, Article, BlogPosting, LocalBusiness, FAQPage and other schema; check syntax, visible facts and current feature support separately. Keep useful FAQs and identify purpose-built, search-intent-led questions even if no customer asked them verbatim. Missing useful FAQ coverage or matching FAQPage questions and answers on appropriate public pages is a baseline gap, not optional. Flag substantive new copy for approval. Preserve valid schema despite one platform withdrawing a feature; never promise rich results or invent reviews, ratings, authors, prices, availability, credentials or facts.

Sitemaps, robots and links
Inspect /sitemap.xml, sitemap indices and /robots.txt. Include intended canonical indexable production URLs, static and dynamic; exclude private, redirected, broken, noindex and unnecessary parameter URLs. Use genuine lastmod, not invented dates or meaningless priority/changefreq. Review navigation, breadcrumbs, pagination, internal links and architecture. Compare sitemap/CMS inventory against reachable links for orphan candidates; use bounded language-aware exact/near-duplicate analysis where scale warrants. Do not infer absence from an incomplete crawl. robots.txt is not authentication. Consider supported update notifications such as IndexNow.

AI and answer discovery
Assess Google, Bing, OpenAI, Anthropic and relevant other engines. Distinguish search, training and user-triggered crawlers; check actual responses, robots, headers, CDN, snippet policies and available logs. Check effective Google generative AI inclusion control, including inheritance, where accessible. Sample comparable queries across relevant engines and locales; record date, settings, brand mentions, cited URLs and competing sources. Distinguish impressions, citations, visits and outcomes. Assess answer clarity, original evidence, credible sources and entity authority; use research-backed techniques without promising citations or treating a heuristic score as probability.

Required llms.txt
Always verify /llms.txt status, final URL, actual intended text/Markdown body and important linked targets; HTTP 200 HTML fallback is not a pass. Check canonical public destinations and stale, private, invented or development links. Recommend creation or maintenance when missing or deficient, including prelaunch readiness. This mandatory baseline is not removed because one engine ignores it; never claim guaranteed rankings or citations.

Content, brand and keywords
Learn brand voice, terminology and intent. Assess definitions, direct answers, purposeful FAQs, comparisons, original evidence, supported claims and topic gaps. For important URLs record primary/supporting phrase counts, approximate density, extraction scope, denominator, variants and placement in title, headings and body. Use sourced industry/practitioner benchmarks and comparable pages to propose justified page-specific density and content-depth targets; assess naturalness, intent and over/underuse, never pad or stuff. Compare independent studies, practitioner experiments, community findings, competitors, first-party evidence and current platform documentation; distinguish standards, benchmarks, associations, causal results and hypotheses. Recommend practical actions/tests, not invented metrics or universal ranking thresholds. Show copy and reasons before approval; no edits.

Entity consistency and specialist checks
Check entity names across headings, navigation, copy, breadcrumbs and schema. Ecommerce: product/variant facts, identifiers, offers, images, collections, feeds, intentional indexable facets versus crawl traps, dedicated landing pages and purchasing/shipping/returns answers. Local: reconcile verified identity, categories, listings/duplicates, locations, hours, service areas, credentials, associations, local schema and authentic photos. Multilingual: locale routes, canonical and reciprocal hreflang with correct language codes. Skip irrelevant checks.

HTML, media, performance and freshness
Review headings, semantic links, media quality, dimensions and loading. Informative images need concise accurate contextual alt text; decorative/redundant images need alt=""; linked images/buttons need action/destination names without duplication; charts need accessible explanation/data. Check galleries, distinct variants, icons, logos, images of text, CMS/localised values and responsive images. Flag missing, generic, filename-only, misleading, wrongly empty, needless repetition or stuffing; never invent unseen details. Review context, captions, filenames and crawlable important images, not CSS alone. Assess LCP, INP, CLS and layout shifts, distinguishing lab from field data. Review genuine freshness and sitemap lastmod; avoid unjustified redesigns.

Audit evidence and report
Use authorised exports or supported read-only APIs for Search Console queries, indexed URL Inspection/selected canonical and Links report, Bing Webmaster/AI Performance, GA4, CrUX and logs when available. Record property, date, dimensions, sampling/quotas and missing data; never confuse indexed inspection with live fetch or infer real crawler identity from UA alone. For traffic declines, locate change point; segment comparable periods by page type, query, device and market; assess tracking, demand/seasonality, deployments/migration parity, crawl/index/canonical changes, manual/security actions, competition and content decay before assigning cause. Run permitted local checks. Give stable finding IDs and preserve all affected URLs, evidence, confidence, impact, effort, platforms, remedy, dependencies and approvals. Group Critical, High, Medium, Low or Optional, Content requiring approval and Information needed. Separate confirmed faults, mandatory llms.txt/FAQ/density gaps and hypotheses; prioritise by business value and evidence. Account for every applicable mandatory criterion as pass, issue, not assessed or justified not applicable; no silent omissions or full pass with missing evidence. No project or account changes.
