Implement the SEO and AEO improvements approved for this website. Support new and existing projects with any capable coding assistant. Optimise for Google, Bing, other search engines and AI answer systems without treating one platform as the sole authority. Approved project rules and later user decisions take precedence. Under this library's standing policy, /llms.txt, site FAQ coverage with matching FAQPage markup and keyword-density checks remain required SEO/AEO baseline components. Do not downgrade them to optional solely because a particular search platform does not use them. Preserve existing functionality, styling, routes, forms, integrations and business logic.

Confirm scope
Inspect the project, audit, approved findings and SEO setup. Link changes to approved IDs or explicit instructions; obtain missing approval, not speculative edits. Reuse current documents, brand rules and validation. For prelaunch work do not claim live indexing. Compare relevant official guidance, independent studies, practitioner results, communities, competing pages and available project evidence; retain useful benchmarks with context.

Plan safe changes
Identify affected URLs/templates, metadata sources, content integrations and deployment boundaries. Prefer framework-native capabilities; avoid duplicate SEO systems. Record risks, dependencies and rollback for redirects, canonicals, indexing and domain changes. For migration map old/new URLs, intent, links, redirects, canonicals and sitemap parity; verify before/after and plan monitoring. Seek approval for architecture, permission or standard changes.

Production versus staging
Use approved production origin for canonicals, schema, sitemap, social URLs and links; exclude localhost/staging. Handle HTTPS, hosts, slashes, parameters and redirects without loops. Development, test and preview environments must not be indexed. Prefer authentication/network restrictions for sensitive sites. Intentional public review previews require reliable noindex, exclusion from production discovery and suitable bot/access controls; disclose residual crawl risk. robots disallow alone cannot ensure deindexing and may hide noindex. Never block production or authorised reviewers; report required CDN/DNS/hosting changes.

Approved technical SEO
Implement relevant approved changes to HTTP statuses, redirects, canonicals, indexing, robots, X-Robots-Tag, crawlable navigation, internal links, semantics, images, performance, international and local SEO or ecommerce. Preserve CMS, API, database and spreadsheet integrations and dynamic data. Do not hardcode changing business information to simplify metadata. For missing routes, create a useful, accessible, branded 404 page with navigation and a real HTTP 404 status. Redirect only when a genuine replacement exists; avoid soft 404s.

Metadata and social cards
Use natural distinct titles, H1 intent and descriptions aligned with brand. Page Topic | Brand is optional. Implement individual applicable Open Graph, X/Twitter Card and canonical fields, verified production URLs and real page-specific images or approved fallback.

Schema and FAQs
Implement relevant accurate Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Product, Article, BlogPosting, LocalBusiness, FAQPage and other approved schema. Maintain helpful purpose-built FAQs based on intent even when no customer asked verbatim, with visible truthful questions and answers matching valid FAQPage markup. This is required; obtain approval for material new copy or claims. Never promise rich results or invent authors, prices, availability, reviews, credentials or facts; verify syntax and current supported feature requirements separately.

Sitemap, robots and updates
Use the project's existing sitemap generation where possible. Include only intended canonical indexable production URLs, covering relevant static and dynamic pages. Exclude private, broken, redirected, noindex, staging and unnecessary parameter URLs. Use reliable lastmod values, never fabricated dates or meaningless changefreq and priority. Implement approved robots policy; it is not an access-control system. Add IndexNow or another suitable update notification only if approved and supported by existing content update events.

AI search access and required llms.txt
Apply approved search, training, user-triggered crawler and snippet policies separately; verify effective inherited AI inclusion controls where available. No external change without approval. Maintain accurate /llms.txt with important canonical public links; verify actual text body, final response and linked targets, not HTML fallback. For prelaunch prepare verified production information, no placeholders, private or staging links. Keep current even if a platform ignores it. No ranking/citation guarantees.

Content changes need approval
Change visible copy, headings, titles, FAQ answers, descriptions, CTAs, anchors or navigation only as authorised. Preserve brand and natural language. For approved pages compare primary/supporting phrase counts, denominator, density, variants and placement before/after; use justified page-specific targets from sourced benchmarks, comparable pages and intent. Retain useful research-backed answer formats and evidence, but no padding, stuffing, false urgency, invented facts or universal ranking targets. Flag missing facts and use existing content governance.

Links, HTML and media
Use crawlable links, useful anchors, headings, semantic landmarks and accessible controls. Informative images need concise accurate context-specific alt text and meaningful distinct gallery/variant descriptions; decorative/redundant images need alt="". Linked images/buttons need destination/action names without duplication; diagrams need accessible explanation/data. Preserve language/brand and use only verified visual facts; no generic filename/product-name alt templates. Reuse CMS/component fields, verify dynamic values and important crawlable images with context, and preserve design.

Conditional site features
Ecommerce: approved real product/variant schema, offers, availability, identifiers, collections, feeds and intentional faceted URL policy. Local: verified listings, locations, services and credentials. Multilingual: locale/canonical and correctly labelled reciprocal hreflang. For approved programmatic SEO, establish demand, data accuracy/rights, unique standalone value, eligible templates and missing-data exclusions; release a small pilot with segmented sitemap, test indexability/render/schema, monitor coverage/errors/outcomes and apply agreed stop/expand/rollback gates before scaling. No fixed page-count or word-count rule; no thin doorway pages. Skip irrelevant modules.

Tests and verification
Run relevant local lint/type/build/tests and validators, not mandatory hosted CI. Check affected home, landing, category, product, article, missing-item and locale templates, initial and rendered HTML/content/links, permitted bot access and separate crawler policies. Verify titles/H1, each applicable social field, canonicals, schema/visible FAQ parity, redirects, robots, sitemap, actual llms.txt body/targets, staging and alt text across galleries, decorative and linked images. Verify numerical keyword evidence before/after. Confirm working forms, navigation, media and integrations. Account for every applicable mandatory criterion by URL/template as pass, issue, not assessed or justified not applicable; preserve all affected URLs. Distinguish local from deployed evidence, never claim unrun checks passed.

Release and final report
Do not deploy or change DNS, CDN, hosting, Search Console, Bing Webmaster, Merchant Center or other external settings without approval. Report approved IDs, changed files/URLs, before/after evidence, tests, unresolved risks, rollback and post-deployment checks. Monitor available search/indexing, repeatable AI query/citation samples, referrals and qualified outcomes, with comparable windows, scope and data limits. Distinguish impressions, citations, visits and business outcomes; do not infer causation from timing alone. No ranking or citation guarantees. Stop for material scope changes.
